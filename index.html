What we do

Every kind of roof the mountains throw at you

Snow load, sun, wind, and monsoon. We install roofs built for the White Mountains climate — and we repair the ones that weren't.

Roof Replacement

Full tear-off and new roof, done clean and on schedule. We handle disposal, flashing, venting, and the details that keep water out for decades.

Roof Repair & Leaks

Missing shingles, storm damage, flashing failures, mystery leaks. We find the real cause and fix it — with an honest call on repair vs. replace.

Metal Roofing

Standing seam and metal panel systems that shed snow, resist wind, and last 40+ years. The right choice for a lot of mountain homes.

Asphalt Shingle Roofing

Architectural shingles installed to manufacturer spec as an Owens Corning member. Great looks, solid warranty, sensible price.

Flat & Low-Slope Roofs

TPO and torch-down membranes for flat and low-slope sections. Properly sealed seams and drainage so water goes where it should.

New Construction & Sealant

Roofing for new builds and additions, plus roof sealant application to extend the life of what you already have.

About Top That Roofing

Family owned. Locally rooted. Roofs done right.

Top That Roofing LLC is a family-owned and operated contracting company based in Snowflake, Arizona, with more than 20 years in roofing and construction. We've spent decades on roofs across the White Mountains, and we know exactly what this climate does to a home.

Customer satisfaction is our highest priority. That means showing up when we say we will, telling you the truth about what your roof needs, and standing behind the work.
